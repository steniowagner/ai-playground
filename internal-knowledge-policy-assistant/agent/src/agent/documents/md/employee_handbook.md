# Employee Handbook

## Employment and conduct

Employees must follow the company Code of Conduct, protect confidential information, and complete all mandatory training. Questions about employment terms should be directed to People Operations. Local employment agreements and applicable law take precedence where they provide greater protection.

## Working hours

The standard workweek is 40 hours. Teams may establish core collaboration hours between 10:00 and 15:00 in the employee's primary working timezone. Employees are expected to keep their calendar and availability status current.

## Flexible work

Employees may work from an approved home location subject to the Remote Work Policy. Work from another country is governed separately by the International Remote Work Policy and always requires advance approval. This handbook does not itself grant permission for international work.

## Time off

Vacation, sick leave, and statutory leave are described in the PTO Policy and applicable local benefits guide. Managers should not discourage employees from taking earned leave.

## Reporting concerns

Suspected harassment, discrimination, retaliation, fraud, or serious misconduct may be reported to People Operations, the Legal team, or the confidential ethics channel. Managers who receive such a report must forward it promptly and must not investigate it independently.
