# Remote Work Policy

## Approved work location

Employees may work remotely from the home address recorded in the HR system when their role is designated remote or hybrid. A permanent move must be approved by the employee's manager and People Operations before the move because payroll, tax, and employment obligations may change.

## Temporary domestic work

An employee may temporarily work elsewhere in the same country for up to 20 working days in a rolling 12-month period with manager approval. The employee must maintain reliable internet access, privacy, and normal working hours. More than 20 days requires People Operations review.

## International work

Working from another country is not covered by the domestic allowance. Employees must follow the International Remote Work Policy even when the trip is personal or shorter than one week.

## Security requirements

Remote workers must use a company-managed device, enable screen locking, connect through the corporate VPN when accessing internal systems, and avoid discussing confidential matters in public spaces. Lost equipment must be reported to Security immediately.
