# Data Classification and Handling Policy

## Public

Public data is approved for release outside the company. Material is not Public merely because it is accessible to many employees; Communications, Legal, or the designated owner must approve publication.

## Internal

Internal data is intended for employees and approved workers. It may be shared through company-managed collaboration systems but not posted publicly. Examples include routine procedures, internal announcements, and non-sensitive project plans.

## Confidential

Confidential data could cause material harm if disclosed. Examples include customer contracts, source code, security architecture, non-public financial results, and detailed employee records. Access must be limited to a business need and transmission must use approved encrypted systems.

## Restricted

Restricted data requires the strongest controls. Examples include authentication secrets, encryption keys, government identifiers, payment-card data, and certain health or biometric data. Restricted data must not be copied into general chat, ordinary tickets, analytics tools, or development environments. Storage requires Security-approved systems and documented access controls.

## Disposal and incidents

Data must be retained only for an approved business or legal purpose and disposed of through approved methods. Suspected misclassification, unintended sharing, or loss must be reported to Security immediately.
