# Customer Support Escalation Guide

## Priority levels

P1 applies when most customers cannot use a critical function, there is credible risk of data loss, or a security compromise is suspected. P2 applies to substantial impact affecting multiple customers without immediate data risk. P3 covers limited issues with an available workaround. P4 covers questions and minor defects.

## Escalation path

P1 cases must be escalated immediately to the support duty manager and engineering on-call; the engineering responder determines whether to declare an incident. P2 cases must be escalated to the owning engineering team within 30 minutes. P3 and P4 cases follow the normal queue and service targets.

## Information to include

The escalation should include the customer and tenant identifiers, observed and expected behavior, timestamps with timezone, scope of impact, reproduction steps, relevant request identifiers, and troubleshooting already performed. Do not place passwords, API keys, payment data, or unnecessary personal data in a support ticket.

## Customer communication

Support may acknowledge impact and provide approved status information but must not speculate about causes, promise resolution times, admit liability, or disclose another customer's information. Security incidents must be coordinated with Security and Communications.
