# Business Travel Policy

## Authorization and booking

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. International business travel also requires the regional Finance partner. Flights, rail, hotels, and rental cars must be booked through the approved travel portal unless the portal cannot serve the route.

## Air and rail

Economy class is required for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or more with director approval. Business class requires an executive-approved medical accommodation or a scheduled flight longer than ten hours.

## Hotels

The nightly room-rate limit is USD 250 before taxes in standard locations and USD 350 before taxes in designated high-cost cities. The travel portal identifies high-cost cities. When no compliant safe hotel is available within a reasonable commute, the traveler must obtain Finance approval before booking above the limit.

## Ground transportation

Public transport or standard ride-hailing should be used when safe and practical. Rental cars require a documented business need. Luxury vehicles are not reimbursable.

## Changes and personal travel

Employees may extend a business trip for personal reasons if there is no additional company cost. Personal nights, companion expenses, and fare differences must be paid personally. Travel disruptions should be handled through the travel provider's emergency channel.
