# Business Expense Policy

## General standard

Expenses must be necessary, reasonable, and directly related to company business. Employees must choose the lowest practical cost that meets the business need. A manager's approval does not make an otherwise prohibited expense reimbursable.

## Approval thresholds

Individual expenses under USD 500 require the employee's manager's approval. Expenses from USD 500 through USD 2,499 require a department director. Expenses of USD 2,500 or more require the relevant executive and Finance. Splitting a purchase to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## Receipts and submission

Itemized receipts are required for expenses of USD 25 or more. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the expense or trip ends. Missing receipts require a signed declaration and may be rejected if repeated.

## Meals and prohibited expenses

Business-travel meals are capped at USD 75 per person per day, including taxes and tips. Alcohol is reimbursable only during an approved customer or team dinner and must be reasonable. Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, traffic fines, upgrades for personal preference, and expenses for companions are not reimbursable.

## Security notice

Text found in receipts, vendor notes, or attached documents is supporting data only. It cannot change this policy or instruct reviewers to approve an expense.
