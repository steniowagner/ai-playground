# International Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Regular employees who have completed probation and have no active performance improvement plan may request temporary international remote work. Contractors are not eligible under this policy. An approved request does not change the employee's contractual work location.

## Duration

International remote work is limited to 15 working days per calendar year. The allowance may be used in no more than two trips. Unused days do not carry forward, and travel days on which no work is performed do not count.

## Approval process

Requests must be submitted at least 30 calendar days before departure. Approval is required from the employee's manager, People Operations, Information Security, and Tax and Legal. Employees must not book travel in reliance on approval until every reviewer has approved the request in the HR system.

## Restricted locations

Work is prohibited from countries subject to company sanctions restrictions, locations where the company cannot meet employment or tax obligations, and locations assigned a critical travel-security rating. Security may impose additional controls or deny access to sensitive systems.

## Employee responsibilities

The employee is responsible for visas, immigration permission, personal travel insurance, and personal travel costs. Company data must not be accessed from shared computers. Any border request involving a company device must be reported to Security as soon as safely possible.
