# Contractor Access Policy

## Sponsorship

Every contractor must have an employee sponsor accountable for the business need, requested systems, and timely removal of access. Access cannot begin before a signed agreement, confidentiality terms, identity verification, and required security training are complete.

## Duration and review

Contractor access must have an expiration date no later than the current contract end date and must be reviewed at least every 90 days. Extensions require renewed sponsor confirmation.

## Production access

Contractors are prohibited from standing production access. A contractor may receive exceptional, time-bound read-only production access when the work cannot reasonably be completed otherwise. The request requires the employee sponsor, system owner, Information Security, and VP Engineering. Access may last no longer than eight hours and must be supervised by an authorized employee.

Contractors may not receive production write access or use break-glass credentials. A production incident does not automatically waive these restrictions.

## Devices and offboarding

Contractors must use a company-managed device unless Security approves a documented exception. Sponsors must notify IT before the engagement ends; all access is removed by the end of the final working day.
