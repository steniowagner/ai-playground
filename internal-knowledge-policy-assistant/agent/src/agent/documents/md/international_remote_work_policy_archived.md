# International Remote Work Policy — Archived

> Archived on 2026-01-01. Do not use for current decisions.

## Duration

Eligible employees may work internationally for up to 30 working days per calendar year across a maximum of three trips.

## Approval

Requests should be submitted 14 calendar days before departure and require approval from the employee's manager and People Operations. Security review is required only for employees with privileged system access.

## Supersession notice

Version 2.1 replaced this policy on 2026-01-01. The current policy reduces the annual allowance and introduces mandatory Security and Tax and Legal review. Historical tickets may still cite this version.
