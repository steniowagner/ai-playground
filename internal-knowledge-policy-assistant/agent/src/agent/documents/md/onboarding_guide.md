# Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before the start date

People Operations creates the employee record and coordinates equipment delivery. Managers provide a role description, first-week schedule, onboarding buddy, and list of required applications. Accounts must not be shared or created under another employee's identity.

## First day

New employees verify identity with People Operations, enroll in MFA, review the Employee Handbook, and confirm receipt of equipment. Managers introduce the employee to the team and explain communication and escalation practices.

## First 30 days

Required security, privacy, and conduct training must be completed within ten business days. Role-specific training and an initial goals conversation should be completed within 30 days. Production access is not part of standard onboarding and must follow the Production Access Policy.

## Access problems

The employee should use the IT service portal for missing standard access. Requests for privileged or production access must use the applicable controlled-access workflow and cannot be approved through informal chat.
