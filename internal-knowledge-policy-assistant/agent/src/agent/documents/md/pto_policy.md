# Paid Time Off Policy

## Vacation entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a prorated entitlement. New hires receive a prorated allocation beginning on their start date.

## Requests

Employees should submit planned leave in the HR system. Leave of five or fewer consecutive working days should normally be requested seven days in advance. Longer leave should normally be requested 30 days in advance. Managers may decline particular dates for documented business coverage reasons but should offer alternatives.

## Carryover

Employees may carry a maximum of five unused vacation days into the following calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31 or they expire, unless local law requires otherwise. Additional carryover requires written approval from the Head of People Operations before year-end.

## Sick leave

Employees who are unwell should notify their manager as soon as practical and record the absence according to local procedure. Medical details should be shared only with People Operations where required, not with the employee's manager.
